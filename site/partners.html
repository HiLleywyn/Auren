The first market

Right terrain.
Reachable internet.

Good first candidate

Clustered rural homes

Open terrain, useful 2–5 km paths, willing site hosts, and diverse terrestrial handoffs. A real adoption estimate and paid operations matter as much as a radio link.

Needs a different plan

Forest, ocean, no upstream

Ground relays do not supply arbitrary forest penetration, aviation service or open-ocean coverage. If every reachable terrestrial gateway is lost, there is no wider internet.

A valid outcome

Use the simpler network

If an existing tower, conventional WISP or fiber extension delivers better lifecycle value, use it. The mechanism must earn its place through measured savings.

Before you enquire

Straight answers.

Can I buy Auren broadband or a mast today?

No. This is an experimental design and working planning software. Feasibility work or development partnerships need a separately agreed scope and available staffing. There is no checkout or hardware preorder.

Is the quarter-cost claim proven?

No. The reference community model is approximately 33% of comparable purchased Starlink service over five years. The mature-production quarter-cost case requires manufacturing and maintenance gains that have not been demonstrated. Read the cost boundary.

Does the published code control a physical mast?

No. The maintenance planner is simulation-only. Public hosting never exposes equipment controls. A real structure needs independently reviewed hardware safety, engineering, permits, procedures and local supervision.

What happens if the concept fails a test?

Stop that deployment path, redesign only with evidence, or use a conventional network. A documented no-go is a useful deliverable. The cheapest first falsifier is a height survey with a rented certified mast.

Can I reuse or manufacture the design?

The repository is source-visible with commercial rights retained. It is not a general open-source, patent or manufacturing license. The CAD is not a construction drawing. Read the rights notice.

The first market

Choosing A First Site

Good first candidate

Clustered Rural Homes

Open terrain, useful 2–5 km paths, willing site hosts, and diverse terrestrial handoffs. We also need a realistic subscriber estimate and a plan for paid operations.

Needs a different plan

Forest, Ocean, No Upstream

This ground network is not a solution for dense forest, aircraft, or the open ocean. It also needs a working terrestrial connection to reach the wider internet.

A valid outcome

Use The Simpler Network

If an existing tower, conventional WISP or fiber extension delivers better lifecycle value, use it. Auren needs to show a measurable advantage before it is worth using.

A scoped program

Pilot Stages

  1. 01

    Site Feasibility

    Survey the sites and test the links in both directions. Get subscriber estimates, permits, and quotes for backhaul, conventional wireless, and fiber. Use those results to decide whether the pilot is worth pursuing.

    Before fabrication / written scope and budget
  2. 02

    Mechanism & Network Experiment

    Test the mechanism in a restrained, low-height rig first. If it passes the safety review, compare the experimental relays with conventional structures under load and during maintenance.

    After safety review / measured test gates
  3. 03

    Qualified Community Pilot

    Run through seasonal weather, record outages and maintenance, and pay for support. An independent review of cost, safety, and service quality should decide whether retail deployment makes sense.

    After evidence review / no promised launch date

Before You Start

Common Questions

Can I Buy Auren Broadband Or A Mast Today?

Not yet. We have an experimental design and planning software. A feasibility study or development partnership would need an agreed scope and people available to do the work.

Is The Quarter-Cost Claim Proven?

No. The reference community estimate is about 33% of comparable purchased Starlink service over five years. Reaching 25% would require manufacturing and maintenance savings we haven’t demonstrated. Read the cost boundary.

Does The Published Code Control A Physical Mast?

No. The planner simulates maintenance. It has no connection to motors or network equipment. A real mast would need independent engineering review, permits, safety procedures, and local supervision.

What Happens If The Concept Fails A Test?

We stop, change the design if the results justify it, or use a conventional network. The first check is a height survey with a rented certified mast.

Can I Reuse Or Manufacture The Design?

The repository is source-visible with commercial rights retained. It is not a general open-source, patent or manufacturing license. The CAD is not a construction drawing. Read the rights notice.

## Evidence labels

- **Reported:** primary publisher statement/result, with date and limits.
- **Calculated:** deterministic model output from explicit inputs.
- **Estimated:** unquoted or unmeasured assumptions.
- **Hypothesis:** the proposed mechanism or cost reduction that must be tested.
- **Measured Auren:** reserved for traceable field observations; none exist in v0.1.

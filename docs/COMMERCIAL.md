# Auren commercial programme

## Positioning

Broadband, closer to earth. Satellite-free adaptive-height relays, terrestrial internet gateways, and repair at ground level. Experimental engineering seeking real sites and partners—not a ready-to-ship Starlink replacement.

## Current offer

Enquiries for scoped feasibility studies and development partnerships. Suitable partners: rural ISPs, community networks, land/site hosts, mechanical fabricators, radio/optical specialists and pilot sponsors. No retail broadband subscription, certified mast sale or hardware preorder is available. No checkout, deposits or card collection exists.

Scope and price require a written quotation and agreement. No fixed delivery date, staffed service capacity or contract acceptance is promised by the website. Do not advertise unstaffed paid services as immediately fulfillable.

## Qualification questions

- General region, approximate home count and terrain type (no home addresses in public issues).
- Existing connectivity, typical service goals, congestion and outages.
- Candidate permitted elevated sites and owner participation.
- Available terrestrial gateways and physical diversity evidence.
- Willingness to benchmark fiber and an ordinary WISP.
- Proposed partner contribution: site, fabrication, funding, operations or instrumentation.

Collect detailed personal/site data only through an agreed private process with appropriate consent and retention policy. Website email links open the visitor's own mail client; no form database exists.

## Sales stages

1. Incoming inquiry, manually reviewed by the owner.
2. Non-binding qualification conversation.
3. Written scope, staffing, permissions, budget, data boundaries and measurable deliverables.
4. Owner acceptance and conventional invoicing, only when capable of delivery.
5. Measured study/pilot and evidence review.
6. No-go, revised design, or qualified next stage. Failed feasibility is a valid result.

No unsolicited outreach has been sent; no prospect, purchase, signed agreement, booking or revenue is claimed. Publishing a sales presence is not making a sale.

## Approved claims

Can say: working software calculations and synthetic failover simulator; explicit test plans; no satellite backhaul in proposed architecture; source-visible engineering; aiming to reduce rural infrastructure cost.

Cannot say: proven 75% cost saving; deployed 100 Mbps Auren service; annual 99.9% availability; global ocean/aviation equivalence; certified structure; patent protection; existing customers/partners; guaranteed delivery.

## Manufacturing target, not a price promise

Short repetitive bays, replaceable locking modules, local fabrication, standardized cartridge connectors, recycled fixture designs and assembly at the base are intended cost levers. Get at least two fabrication bids after structural design. Do not reduce locks, anchors, corrosion protection or safety review to meet a spreadsheet target.

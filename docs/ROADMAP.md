# Development roadmap

Small core: mechanical/structural engineer, RF/network engineer, embedded/software engineer, with contracted structural review and field installation. Not an existing staffed company or a delivery commitment.

## First 30 days

Deliverables: twenty-home height survey, written upstream/site quotes, reference WISP/fiber bids, low-height locking/lifting rig, legal bidirectional radio characterization, instrument plan, failure-domain inventory. No taller work before safety review.

Estimated cumulative budget $25k–$40k. Release funding only if height/reusability adds enough qualified demand or maintenance savings.

## First 90 days

Two experimental 12 m relays; 8–20 consenting test homes; benchmark conventional installation; measured congestion/reuse, battery endurance, handover and maintenance time; initial optical bench if separately eye-safe. No annual-availability claim from a few weeks of results.

Estimated cumulative budget $75k–$120k. Gate: structural tests and economic advantage, not merely a working speed test.

## First year

Qualified taller design if viable; 100–240-home pilot; seasonal weather/maintenance data; independent lifecycle review; manufacturable drawings, service manuals, supplier qualification and insurance. Obtain actual operational, privacy and regulatory approvals before retail service.

Estimated cumulative budget $300k–$500k, potentially higher for certification and carrier construction. No purchases, contracts or hires committed by this repository. An owner-approved procurement budget is required before spending.

## Global track (separate)

First test resource, payload-power and fleet-cost assumptions; do not begin with aircraft manufacture. Existing HAPS partners and wind/solar datasets can falsify year-round coverage economics cheaply. Gate airborne work on a credible energy closure, permission plan and capacity-per-dollar model. It is not funded by the regional pilot model.
